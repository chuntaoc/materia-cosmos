開始Getting started

星海航路圖是工程材料課程的互動地圖。每顆星球是一個章節，點進去會看到遊戲介紹，再前往該章的遊戲。Materia Cosmos is the interactive map for the Engineering Materials course. Each planet is a chapter. Open one to watch its game intro, then go to the chapter's game.

  • 在開始畫面選擇語言：中文、中文 / English 或 English。On the start screen, choose a language: Chinese, Chinese / English, or English.
  • 手機或平板建議先按「全螢幕」。On a phone or tablet, tap Full screen first.
  • 按「開始觀測」進入星圖。開場旁白可按右下角「略過」。Tap Start to enter the map. Use Skip at the lower right to skip the opening narration.

航行與遊戲介紹Voyage and game intro

  • 在星球卡片按「啟航」，會播放躍遷動畫。Tap Launch on a planet card to start the warp animation.
  • 接著是約 30 到 45 秒的遊戲介紹：故事、角色、玩法與學習重點。右下角可「略過介紹」。Next comes a game intro of about 30 to 45 seconds: story, characters, how to play and what you will learn. Use Skip intro at the lower right.
  • 抵達後按「踏上星球」進入該章遊戲，這顆星就會記為已登陸。On arrival, tap Land on planet to open the chapter's game. The planet is then marked as landed.

手機與平板Phones and tablets

  • 開始畫面與右上角都有全螢幕按鈕。There is a Full screen button on the start screen and at the top right.
  • iPhone 的 Safari 不支援網頁全螢幕。請用「分享 → 加入主畫面」，從主畫面開啟即可滿版顯示。Safari on iPhone does not support web full screen. Use Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from the home screen.
  • 在部落格文章中看到的是嵌入版本；空間不夠時可按文章下方的「在新分頁開啟」。In the blog post you see an embedded version. If it feels cramped, use Open in a new tab below the post.
  • 覺得卡頓或發熱時，把畫質設為「流暢」，必要時把繪圖改為「軟體」。If it stutters or the phone gets warm, set Quality to Smooth, and switch Rendering to Software if needed.

常見問題Troubleshooting

狀況Problem處理方式What to do
沒有聲音No sound瀏覽器要求先點一下畫面才能播放聲音。確認設定中的音效或背景音樂已開啟，並檢查手機靜音開關。Browsers only play sound after a tap. Check that effects or music is on in Settings, and check the phone's silent switch.
